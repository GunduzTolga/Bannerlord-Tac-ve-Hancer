# Popular Single-Player Gameplay, Campaign-Overhaul and QoL Mods for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord (as of 18 Oct 2026) — and the Vanilla Gaps They Fill

Scope notes for the report writer:
- Single-player only. Pure cheat/editor mods and pure armor/cosmetic packs are excluded from the catalog (they appear only in the popularity rankings so the ranking is complete, flagged as "excluded").
- Data was read directly from Nexus Mods mod pages (header stats: endorsements, unique/total downloads, version, "Last updated") and Steam Workshop pages on 18 Oct 2026. "X months ago" style dates come from Nexus listing tiles and are relative to 18 Oct 2026.
- **Version-number trap:** Bannerlord's Early Access builds were numbered e1.0–e1.9 (2020–2022); the full release restarted at v1.0.0 (Oct 2022). So a mod that says "1.4.x" and was last updated in 2020 targets EA **e1.4**, not the 2026 **v1.4.x**. Mod self-versions (e.g. Banner Kings "1.4.0.2") are also not game versions. Every compatibility claim below was checked against the "Last updated" date.
- **Current game version context:** The stable branch as of Oct 2026 is v1.4.8 (a 160+ mod guide updated 11 Oct 2026 says "Notes are built on game version 1.4.8 (1.4.7 also works well)" — [Vanilla 1.4.8 Plus Modding Notes](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/11354)); a v1.5.x beta exists (Bellum Civile "targets Bannerlord 1.4.8. Added experimental version recompiled for 1.5.5 Beta" — [Bellum Civile](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/10609)). War Sails launched on the v1.3.x line (TaleWorlds patch notes titled "WS v1.0.3 / BL v1.3.7" and "WS v1.1.1 / BL v1.3.13" — [TaleWorlds News 590](https://www.taleworlds.com/en/News/590), [TaleWorlds News 596](https://www.taleworlds.com/en/News/596); titles seen via search, page bodies behind a bot check).

## Q1. What are the all-time most endorsed / most downloaded Bannerlord mods on Nexus, and the most subscribed on Steam Workshop?

### Takeaway
Across both platforms the top non-library slots are held by the same handful of campaign/QoL mods — Improved Garrisons, Diplomacy, Detailed Character Creation, Realistic Battle Mod, Better Time, Serve as Soldier — sitting directly under the BUTR library stack (MCM, ButterLib, UIExtenderEx, Harmony, BLSE), which is itself the most-endorsed content on Nexus because nearly every code mod depends on it.

### Inferences
- The two platforms agree strongly: Diplomacy and Improved Garrisons are #4/#5 on Steam (right after the three libraries) and #5/#4 on Nexus. Those two plus RBM, Better Time, DCC and Xorberax's Legacy form a de-facto "core" modded experience.
- Steam's top-90 is noticeably more QoL-skewed than Nexus' (Heal on Kill, Bodyguards, Quick Nearby Tournaments, Remove Fog of War, Stop Starving Yourselves, Workshops Completed, You Keep What You Kill, Upgrade Your Equipment) — small single-purpose fixes that Steam's one-click subscribe makes cheap to adopt.
- The past-year Nexus chart is dominated by (a) politics/diplomacy depth (AI Influence, Courier, Bellum Civile, Kingdom Politics Expanded, Titles), (b) "continued/updated for 1.3/1.4" re-uploads of abandoned classics (Party AI Controls 1.3.X, Governors Handle Issues (Updated), Equip Best Item Reloaded, Both Perks 1.3.x, Bandit Militias Redux), and (c) War Sails gap fillers (Helmsman, Ship Ferries).

## Q2. Which popular mods are still updated for v1.3 / v1.4, and which were abandoned?

### Takeaway
Most of the flagship campaign mods (Diplomacy, Improved Garrisons, RBM, Fourberie, Better Time, Xorberax's Legacy, Player Settlements, Retinues, Marry Anyone's continuation, Better Smithing Continued, AutoTrader, Family Control) were updated for v1.4.x in 2026. A large tier of early classics is dead (Bannerlord Tweaks, Party AI Overhaul and Commands, Freelancer, Party Screen Enhancements, Fast Dialogue, Attribute Per Level, Settlement Icons, original Distinguished Service, original Marry Anyone), and several mid-generation mods survive only as third-party "Updated/Reforged/1.3.X" re-uploads — notably carbon198's suite, which stopped at a 28 Nov 2025 release and was taken over by others.

### Inferences
- The modding scene shows a clear "continuation" culture: when a big author leaves, a re-upload with "Updated / Reloaded / Reforged / 1.3.X" in the name appears within weeks (Party AI Controls → 1.3.X → Reforged; Governors Handle Issues → Reloaded → Updated; Marry Anyone → Game 1.5.X; My Little Warband → Reforged; Distinguished Service → 1.3+ Warsails; Bandit Militias → Redux; Equip Best Item → Reloaded 1.4.x). This means the *features* are durable demand signals even when individual mods die.
- War Sails (v1.3) was a mass-breakage event: many mods reset their compatibility claims around Nov 2025–Jan 2026, and some popular authors (carbon198, DCC's maintainer) stepped back around then.
- Even the dependency layer needed patching: an unofficial "Harmony Updated v1.4.6" rebuild reached 197 endorsements in the past year ([Nexus past-year listing p2](https://www.nexusmods.com/games/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods?sort=endorsements&timeRange=365&page=2)).

### Gaps
- Improved Garrisons, AutoTrader and Better Time pages do not state an explicit "tested on 1.4.x" line; their 2026 update dates are the only evidence of 1.4 compatibility.
- Banner Kings' actual compatibility with v1.3/1.4 could not be confirmed (its GitHub/Discord were not checked).

### Inferences
- About half of the stated gaps are **management-load** gaps (fiefs, governors, clan parties, trade, party screen, inventory) — the game scales the number of things the player owns faster than the tools to manage them.
- The other half are **content/agency** gaps (diplomacy options, politics, careers, roleplay, family, settlement building) — mods add verbs the player cannot do in vanilla.
- Several gaps exist as hidden or buggy vanilla systems that mods merely switch on or fix (DCC's "debug values", native inventory search, T5/T6 prisoner recruitment bug), which suggests cheap wins for any successor mod or official patch.

### Gaps
- No currently popular "Destiny"-named mod was found on Nexus; adoption mods exist but are small. The "Destiny/Adopt" lead from the brief could not be verified.
- No widely endorsed non-cheat fast-travel mod exists on Nexus (keyword "travel" returns only tiny mods, max 31 endorsements; teleport mods belong to the cheat/editor researcher). Better Time (map speed) is the practical substitute.
- No current, popular successor to Bannerlord Tweaks was found. Modern equivalents appear to be many single-purpose mods (Better Core / Better Attributes / XP Tweaks per the 1.4.8 guide), but they were not individually verified.
- Dependencies for listing-only mods (marked "not checked") were not verified.

## Q4. Which mods are commonly called "should be in vanilla"?

### Takeaway
Diplomacy and Improved Garrisons are the mods most often described as base-game material, alongside a recurring Warband-era wishlist (messengers, bank, custom troop trees, building fiefs). TaleWorlds has absorbed at least two mod features (alliances, "transfer up to limit" in the party screen), which supports the claim. Direct Reddit evidence was thin and could not be read first-hand.

### Cited Findings
- Diplomacy's own feature list marks "Alliances (now in vanilla)" — i.e., a headline mod feature was adopted by TaleWorlds — [Nexus 832](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/832)
- Party Screen Enhancements v1.6.0: "Remove the transfer up to limit button (vanilla functionality now)" — [Nexus 492](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/492)
- A Steam commenter on Diplomacy (Nov 2022): "why is this in a mod...and not in the base game" (quoted by a search-engine summary of Steam discussions; not opened directly) — [Steam Diplomacy discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/2881380744/4783413655226094521)
- A Reddit user (via mirror) argued many Warband mod features "should have been included in Bannerlord. Like diplomacy, sending messengers, a bank system, custom troop tree, building fiefs" (search summary; not verified first-hand) — [r/Bannerlord mirror](https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/Bannerlord/comments/1gpnhvk/warband_or_bannerlord)
- Each of those wishlist items maps to a current top mod: messengers → Diplomacy/Courier/Telepathy; bank → Xorberax's Legacy (Banks of Calradia); custom troop tree → Retinues/My Little Warband; building fiefs → Player Settlements — [Nexus 832](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/832), [9048](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/9048), [3462](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/3462), [8847](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/8847), [7298](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/7298)

- An older r/Bannerlord_MODS post noted vanilla's three conversation tones (polite/neutral/haughty) don't affect relations, and asked for playable minor cultures (search summary) — [r/Bannerlord_MODS mirror](https://nyc1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/Bannerlord_MODS)

### Inferences
- The "should be vanilla" set is effectively: Diplomacy, Improved Garrisons, Party Screen/inventory batch actions, Better Time, Governors Handle Issues, Party AI Controls, a custom-troop-tree mod, and settlement building. These are also the mods most frequently re-uploaded when abandoned (Q2), which reinforces the signal.

### Gaps
- Reddit could not be read directly (the built-in browser blocks reddit.com and WebSearch surfaced few dated threads). No 2025–2026 "should be vanilla" thread was verified; the quotes above come from search summaries and mirror sites.

## Q6 (synthesis). Which feature themes recur across mods — strongest signals of what vanilla lacks?

### Takeaway
Seven themes appear again and again, across all-time rankings, past-year uploads, Steam subscriptions and curated collections: (1) diplomacy and kingdom politics depth, (2) automation of fief/clan/garrison management, (3) control over clan/vassal party AI, (4) player-owned custom troops, (5) alternative careers and peacetime activities, (6) family/companion/relationship depth, and (7) UI/inventory/info QoL plus progression-grind relief. War Sails has added a new eighth cluster: naval QoL.

### Inferences
- The strongest single signal is the **"multi-generation" features**: ones that have been rebuilt 3+ times by different authors across EA → v1.0 → v1.2 → v1.3/1.4 (clan-party control, governor issue handling, both-perks, marry-anyone, custom troop trees, troop-to-companion promotion, equip-best-item, tweak packs). Demand for these has survived six years and several breaking patches.
- For a new mod project, the clearest open spaces are: (a) a maintained, modular all-in-one QoL/tweak pack (no live successor to Bannerlord Tweaks); (b) a 1.4-native freelancer/soldier career with long-term support (SaS is 1.3.15, Enlisted is early/0.9, Freelancer is dead); (c) a 1.4-maintained party/inventory batch-action UI (Party Screen Enhancements died in 2022); (d) non-cheat fast travel or travel QoL; (e) War Sails naval management QoL, which is still thinly covered; (f) feudal/economic depth on 1.4 (Banner Kings' 1.3/1.4 support is unconfirmed).

### Gaps
- Quantitative theme weighting (e.g. summed endorsements per theme) was not computed; the counts above are qualitative.
- Some Steam-only mods (Bodyguards, Heal on Kill, Stop Starving Yourselves, Workshops Completed, Upgrade Your Equipment, You Keep What You Kill, Tutelage, Make Calradia Great Again, AgricultureEstate, Hideout Plus, DynaCulture) were seen only by title in the most-subscribed list; their feature lists and versions were not opened.
